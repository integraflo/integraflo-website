AUTOMATION & INTEGRATIONS

Improve the processes connected to Salesforce.

Automations and integrations should make work easier, not more fragile. When a process fails or new changes expose dependencies, IntegraFlo can investigate the issue and design or deliver an agreed improvement.

Discuss your Salesforce needs

Understand the full process before changing it.

We look at how information moves between Salesforce and connected systems, including Flow, validation rules, APIs and custom development where relevant. We identify dependencies and practical options before recommending changes.

If the issue involves another platform, we work with its owners to determine what needs to change and how to deliver it safely.

DELIVERY

Design for a safer release.

Map the current state

Identify triggers, dependencies, failure points and expected behaviour.

Build and test

Choose an appropriate configuration or development approach and test in a lower environment where practical.

Release deliberately

Review the change, obtain production authorization, verify behaviour and document what matters.
