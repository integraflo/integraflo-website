AUTOMATION & INTEGRATIONS

Improve the processes connected to Salesforce.

Automations and integrations should reduce work for your team. When they fail, overlap or become hard to change, IntegraFlo helps understand the existing process and design a controlled improvement.

Discuss your Salesforce needs

Understand the full process before changing it.

We examine the business outcome, Salesforce configuration, data movement and systems involved. The work may include Flow, validation, APIs, integration behaviour, reporting dependencies or small development tasks where they are needed for the agreed outcome.

We distinguish a Salesforce issue from a connected-system issue and identify the right owner for each part. Scope and release steps are agreed before implementation.
