SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING

Get to the cause of Salesforce issues.

A broken process can affect users long before the cause is clear. IntegraFlo investigates the symptoms, checks the relevant Salesforce configuration and helps identify a safe path to resolution.

Discuss your Salesforce needs

When support needs more than a quick fix.

Examples include user access or permission problems, unexpected record behaviour, Flow errors, reporting discrepancies, and failures involving a connected system. We establish what changed, what is affected and which evidence supports the likely cause.

Where a problem reaches beyond Salesforce, we help define the boundary and coordinate the information needed from the other system's owner.

OUR APPROACH

Investigate, explain and resolve with control.

Understand impact

Clarify affected users, records, processes and urgency before changing anything.

Follow the evidence

Review configuration, automation, data and connected-system signals with least-privilege access.

Agree the fix

Explain findings, test appropriate changes and obtain explicit authorization for production work.
